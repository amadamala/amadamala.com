Calibrating an LLM judge against human labels

Thu, Nov 26, 2026 An LLM judge is a model that reads the output of another model and decides whether the output is good. Teams use LLM judges because people are too slow to grade every output, and exact string matching can’t grade open-ended answers. The problem is that a judge is also a model, and a judge makes mistakes. If you report “92% of answers passed” and the judge is wrong on one answer in five, nobody knows what the 92% means.
